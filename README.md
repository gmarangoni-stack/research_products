## Slide Deck (index.pdf)
For this assignment, I had the following instructions:

**Show off your data analytic research skills** 
- Design and complete a mini original research project with either a short paper or a slide deck as your primary deliverable. 
- You must use Quarto or R Markdown to create your deliverable. 
- Upload your completed project in pdf format.

**Motivation**
- Your project must include:
- Clear topical motivation
- Description of the data 
- Professional visuals
- Appropriate analysis
- Thoughtful conclusion.

**Data** 
- Find a dataset that is meaningful and/or interesting to you
- You may not use anything from class or canned/fake data from instructional or teaching websites
- Your data must have more than 150 observations (preferably much more)
- Beyond that, do what you want. I encourage you to pursue interesting data, even if you need to scrape it yourself.

**Overall**

This should be a professional product in both form and substance
Someone who encounters it should see that you took care in preparing it and should
be able to learn something concrete from your analytic insights

The data used was extracted directly from the  Latinobarómetro data publication for the 2023 wave.
You may access the data in the following link: https://www.latinobarometro.org/latinobarometro-2023
The data and codebook are also included in the repository.

Overall, this study aimed to analyze the question: 
"To what extent does partisanship and presidential approval shape the view of the
Brazilian population towards Foreign Policy, specifically in attitudes towards
United States and China?*"

I used several questions present in the Latinobarómetro survey from 2023, 
including questions in relation to:
- Presidential approval
- U.S. and China favorability 
- Political and economic relations with U.S. and China 
- Great power preference 
- Vote intention / party alignment 

My approach was to create plots with graphs portraying different responses, highlighting
Brazilians' attitudes towards the United States and China. Later, I compared these 
to determine how people viewed Brazil's relationship with these countries. I later 
compared government supporters and the opposition in their responses about Great Powers in order
to have a better perspective on different people's attitudes. I finalized 
the analysis by comparing all of the plots and drawing a conclusion about Brazilians'
opinions on Great Powers and determined what is Brazil's potential future amid 
the upcoming presidential elections in October 2026. 

The slides have the data, methodology, analysis, and overall conclusion in 
relation to the upcoming presidential elections in October 2026


## Report (student_performance)
For this assignment, I had the following instructions:
Teach me something using a dataset of interest to you. Find a dataset, and present a combi-
nation of text (300-500 words), visualization, and relevant statistics that tell a coherent story.

The visual should be excellent. The text should be a sharp explanation of the data, the mea-
sure(s), and the conclusion. A good answer will teach me something as a reader. It will have
a main point, and the visual, prose, and stats will work together to make that point.

The data extracted was from the Student Performance Dataset, in kaggle. The link to the dataset
can be accessed through the following link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kundanbedmutha/student-performance-dataset.
The data can also be found in the repository.

The research topic for this was the relationship between hours studied and exam performance in students. 
I examined the relationship to determine if "A"" students are relatively the more studious or not. 

My approach was to create a plot and include diverse responses in the graph as different 
points and analyze the amount of hours that "A" students dedicated to studying. Also, 
I included a simple linear regression to analyze the relationship between hours studied
and exam performance in students. I ended the short report by providing a conclusion
to the information on the graph and providing additional hypotheses to explain
performance in the students with the best exam scores.
